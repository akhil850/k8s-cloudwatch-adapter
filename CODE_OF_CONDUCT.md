# Code of Conduct

This policy has been rewritten for the independently maintained fork. Conduct matters in this fork are handled by its maintainer, not AWS.

## Expected behavior

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels, give constructive feedback, and keep discussions focused on the work.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, and sharing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable. These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, and other project discussions.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the [maintainer](https://github.com/akhil850) using a contact method listed on their profile to report concerns privately. Do not post sensitive details or personal information in public issues. If no private contact method is available, ask the maintainer to provide one without sharing the incident details publicly.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, close or lock discussions, and restrict participation to protect the community. Reports should be handled respectfully and shared only as needed to investigate and respond.
